      Stay safe while triaging

      • Never click links or open attachments in a suspicious email in order to test them.
      • Verify important messages independently; open the organisation's official website or app yourself, or call a number you already have. Do not use links or numbers taken from the email.
      • Never share passwords, MFA/verification codes or one-time passwords with anyone, no matter who asks.
      • If the email relates to work or study, report it to your IT or security team using your usual internal process.
      • This tool is for learning and triage only, it can never guarantee that an email is safe or malicious.
